## Description

```
Floorcount is a salsa practice Lens for Snap's Specs AR glasses. It puts the step's footprints on your own floor at real size, lights each one on its count, and changes the lesson after every 8-count: two Agains slow it down, three clean 8-counts move you on.

My entry to Learn AR with SPECS on Devpost (November 2026). Built with Snap's CLAD in Lens Studio through the Lens Studio MCP, tested with 18 LEAF scenarios. Everything in the Lens shown here is Lens Studio's preview; it has not run on glasses yet.

Code: https://github.com/JonathanSolvesProblems/floorcount
Write-up: https://jonathanandrei.com/blog/floorcount-salsa-steps-on-your-floor-specs-lens/
```
