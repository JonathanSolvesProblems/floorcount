## Title

```
Salsa steps on your floor: a Lens for Snap's AR glasses
```

## Description

```
I've been learning salsa at a dance school for a couple of years, and practising at home means a tutorial that faces you. Floorcount puts the step on your own floor at real size, lights each print on its count, and changes the lesson after every 8-count.

Shown in Lens Studio's preview; it hasn't run on glasses yet.
Full demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n4pGnXYn0ys
Code: https://github.com/JonathanSolvesProblems/floorcount

Music: "Salsa Island" by Steve Oxen. Music used from https://www.FesliyanStudios.com

#salsa #augmentedreality #SnapSpecs #LensStudio #dance
```
