## Description

```
A tutorial faces you, so every salsa step is mirrored. Floorcount puts the step on your own floor at real size, lights each print on its count, and changes the lesson after every 8-count.

Shown in Lens Studio's preview; it hasn't run on glasses yet.
Full demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n4pGnXYn0ys
Code: https://github.com/JonathanSolvesProblems/floorcount
```
