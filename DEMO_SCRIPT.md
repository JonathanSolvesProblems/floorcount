# Floorcount: demo script (target 75 to 90 seconds; the event allows up to 3 minutes)

Everything in the Lens is a recording of Lens Studio's Interactive Preview. The only real
footage is me at home. No other people, no third-party logos or app branding
on screen (the rules ban third-party trademarks), and no licensed music: the only music is
the practice loop the Lens generates.

`N` in the opener is the number from `devpost/phone-vs-glasses.md`, measured on a real
practice before the build. It is never guessed. If the file does not exist, the opener drops
the number and keeps the sentence.

| Time | Shot | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 s | My room. A propped-up phone plays a salsa tutorial (screen out of focus, audio muted). I start a right turn, lose the count, walk to the phone and scrub back. | "This is how I practise salsa at home. The video faces me, so every step is mirrored. I lose the count and walk back to the phone. N times in [M] minutes." |
| 10 to 25 s | Lens Studio preview. The pad appears on the floor 70 cm ahead with the basic step's sole prints at real size, left feet solid and right feet outlined. The music starts at Slow. Footprints light on 1, 2, 3, hold, 5, 6, 7, hold. The clave clicks on 1 and 5 from the floor. The count number and the strip of eight cells sit above the far edge, the 1 in teal, 4 and 8 empty. | "Floorcount puts the count on my own floor. Each footprint lights on its beat, from where I stand, and the clave comes from where my feet go." |
| 25 to 40 s | I pinch Again after one 8-count and again after the next (the Lens takes one answer per 8-count). The music drops a level and only counts 1 to 4 stay lit. I pinch footprint 5; it flashes. | "When I lose it, I pinch Again. Twice in a row and it slows down and gives me half the step. I can pinch the count I lost, and it remembers." |
| 40 to 55 s | Got it after each of three clean 8-counts at Full. The basic's footprints fade over two counts and the right turn loads, with its arc on counts 2 and 3. I look up and away from both the pad and the card: a small arrow at the bottom of the view points back to the pad and the clave keeps playing. | "Three clean at full speed and it moves on. The count number hides once I have the step, so I am dancing eyes up, to the music. Look away, and a small arrow points back." |
| 55 to 68 s | Ten seconds of the build: the plan-lens brief in `devpost/`, CLAD placing the pad in Lens Studio, a LEAF scenario failing, a design note, the scenario passing. | "I planned it with the Devpost skill pack. Snap's CLAD built it in Lens Studio through the MCP, and every done criterion has a LEAF test that passes." |
| 68 to 85 s | A full right-turn 8-count in the preview: each footprint lights on its count and fades as it lands clean, and the floor goes quiet. Cut to me doing the same turn in my room, eyes up, no phone in frame. | "Right turn, on the one, no rewinding. Floorcount." |

Final shot: the turn, done, in my own room. Not a number being revealed.

Cards: `.design/brand/export/cards/title-card-1920x1080.png` for 2 seconds before the opener, and `end-card-1920x1080.png` for 3 seconds after the final shot. Both come from the brand kit (`.design/brand/`), so the video opens and closes on the same wordmark, strip and walnut floor the Lens icon uses.

Checks before recording: nothing in the climax comes from a seed or fixture; the LEAF pass
shown is a real run; the footwork on screen is the brief's footwork; the phone in the opener
shows no readable branding; the number in the opener traces to `devpost/phone-vs-glasses.md`.
