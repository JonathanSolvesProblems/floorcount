# Floorcount: the demo video

One cut, 2 minutes 58 seconds, under the event's 3-minute limit. The voice is one take I recorded on 8 November 2026, trimmed in two places (the greeting at the start and one aside), and nothing was re-recorded. Reviewers do not run the project, so the video is the only place they see it work and the only place they see how it was built.

Everything in the Lens is a recording of Lens Studio's preview, and the video says so: a "Lens Studio preview" tag sits on every Lens shot, and the limits are stated out loud near the end. The only real footage is me: a salsa class in January 2026 (my partner's head and everyone along the walls blurred), and my home practice on the day of recording. No third-party video and no logos. The music is "Bailarina" by Justin Allan Arnold (ifnessfreemusic.com), under CC BY 4.0, credited on the end card; it ducks under the voice and comes up on the cards.

Every number spoken or shown traces to a source listed in `devpost/sources.md`, or to a measurement in this repo. The opener's card comes from `devpost/phone-vs-glasses.md`, one real practice measured from the phone's screen recording and a room camera.

## How it was made

The scripts are in `video/`. The footage itself (`broll/`) is not in the repository: it is my home and a class, and the raw files run to gigabytes.

- `video/make_video.py` builds it: one segment per row above from the clips in `broll/`, joined with hard cuts (dissolves only into and out of the cards and between the two home shots), overlays and captions on top, the audio mixed in numpy. The voice goes through a high-pass, a light denoise, 3:1 compression and loudness normalisation to -16 LUFS; the music sits about 17 dB under it while I speak and comes up to about -22 LUFS on the cards and in long pauses, with every gain change ramped over a third of a second; the two pushes (into the count strip, and onto the before-and-after card) are sub-pixel affine warps per frame, because ffmpeg's zoompan moves in whole pixels and shakes; an inaudible floor fills any exact silence so no player clicks at the title card. The word-by-word captions are written by the caption writer of my own video tool, vidkit, which it imports.
- `video/cards.py` draws the overlays, the measured-practice card, the floor diagram and the footprints timeline, in the brand kit's palette and type.
- `video/blur_dancers.py` blurs every head but mine in the class clip. A face detector misses heads that are turned away, so it works from body pose instead (YOLO pose with ByteTrack ids): a head box from each person's nose, eyes, ears and shoulders, whichever way they face, and me picked out by the colour of my sweater. A lost head keeps its blur for half a second. It writes a frame grid, checked by eye, and the shot uses only a stretch where every frame came out right. (`video/blur_class.py` is the face-detector version used on an earlier clip.)
- `video/record_terminal.ps1` records the terminal take with the font set large enough to read, and restores the terminal's settings after. `video/transcribe_voice.py` gives the word timings, `video/cuts_sheet.py` and `video/count_rewinds.py` are the measurement tools behind `devpost/phone-vs-glasses.md`, and `video/check_video.py` runs the checks below on the finished file.

## Checks before publishing

Nothing in the climax comes from a seed or fixture. The LEAF run shown is a real run. The terminal shows the real `npm run breaks` output. The phone in the opener shows no readable branding. Every number spoken is on the Devpost page with its source. The second-session beat is a real second session. The class footage shows no face but mine. The music is CC BY 4.0 and credited on screen and in the README. The file is under 3 minutes, 1920 by 1080, 30 fps, AAC at 48 kHz with clean packet timing, and the head of the audio carries no exact digital silence.
