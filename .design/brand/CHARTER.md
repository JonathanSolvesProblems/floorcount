# Charter: Floorcount

Laws every surface obeys: the Lens in the preview, the Lens icon, the video's title card, the Devpost page. Each law is checkable and each has its because. The because is a fact about salsa, the clave, the additive display, or a photo in `.design/refs/NOTES.md`.

## Light
1. **Inside the Lens, nothing meant to be seen is darker than 40% luminance, and black is never a colour.** On the icon, the video title card and the Devpost page, the walnut floor (`floor`, #1A1612) is the ground and nothing else: it never carries a fill, a stroke or text. Because the display adds light to the room: black is nothing, and a dark fill is a dim fill.
2. **Ivory (`strike`, #F7EEDF) is the only ink.** Text, numerals, outlines, footprints, the pad border: all ivory. Hierarchy is brightness, never a second colour. Because on an additive display alpha is brightness, and the studio wall is the brightest thing in the room.
3. **Teal (`uno`, #5FB4E8; on an ivory ground the unlifted eyedrop #4486B2) is on exactly one thing at a time: the 1.** The count-1 cell of the strip and the footprint that lands on 1. Never on text longer than one numeral, never on a button, never as a fill behind anything. Because finding the 1 is the whole job of an On1 dancer.
4. **Only the current count is at full brightness.** Every other footprint and cell rests at 35%. Because the clave's grid has struck boxes and unstruck boxes, and the eye must go to the strike.
5. **Holds (4 and 8) are rests: nothing lights and the strip shows an empty cell.** Never a dimmed numeral, never a greyed footprint. Because a rest in the clave is silence, not a quiet note.

## The floor
6. **Left feet are solid, right feet are outlined, at every brightness, always.** Colour never codes a foot. Because the clave is one pair with two roles, the striker and the held, and because a foot must read mid-turn to anyone, including in a compressed video.
7. **A footprint is a dance-shoe sole print, left and right mirrored, 27 by 10 cm, toes toward the direction of travel.** Never a cartoon foot, never a blob with a numeral inside. Because the footwork-diagram look (peach and light-blue blobs with numbers) is the convention this Lens leaves.
8. **The pad is a bordered square with a faint parquet grain and nothing else drawn on it, and parquet appears only on floor surfaces: the pad, the count band, the icon's ground.** Never on a page background, never behind text. No grid, no centre mark, no compass. The turn arc appears only on its counts. Because the class floor is a portable parquet square with an edge laid over the room, and Snap's own guidance says a faint pattern hides the display's unevenness.

## Motion
9. **A light rises one step on the "and", strikes on the count, and decays over one beat to rest.** Attack is instant. Nothing fades in for feel, nothing moves on exit except the two-count fade between steps the brief specifies. Because a struck clave has no attack time and rings out.
10. **Nothing else animates.** No pulsing buttons, no hover glow, no bounce. Because a dancer's eye must only ever be pulled by the count.

## Type and words
11. **One typeface, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, for everything, and inside the Lens one weight: ExtraBold.** Medium is for long text on a page. Because the dancer reads at 1 m while moving, the face was designed so 1, 7, I and l cannot be confused, and in the preview a Medium stroke washed out against a bright room, where brightness is already spoken for as the count hierarchy.
12. **Numerals are tabular and single: one count per cell, never a range typed as "1-4" in a numeral position.** Because the strip is the clave's grid, one box per beat.
13. **No text smaller than 2.5 cm cap height at 1 m, and no text block longer than two lines.** Because the glasses' view is 32 degrees wide and a glance has to earn its place.
14. **Titles and labels carry no punctuation, no exclamation marks, no emoji, and no praise.** The voice is the teacher counting (`voice.md`). Because the dancer is working.

## Placement and chrome
15. **Two targets and no more: Again on the left, Got it on the right, capsules of equal width, at least 12 cm and fitted to their labels with 6 cm between them.** Neither is the primary: the dancer reports, the Lens does not nudge. One answer per 8-count. No icon rows, no menus, no settings, no close buttons. Because hands are a noisy input and pinch is the new tap.
16. **Panels are never opaque and never dark: at most 30% ivory, and never wider than the pad.** The step card is ivory text on the air with a 30% capsule behind the buttons only. Because a frosted dark panel floating at eye level is the SnapOS default and the event's own named failure.
17. **Nothing sits above eye level or behind the user.** The off-view arrow is the only element allowed at the edge of the view, and only while neither the pad nor the step card is in view. Because content lives on the thing it is about, and the thing is the floor.

## The signature device
18. **The clave strip (eight capsule cells, holds empty, the 1 in teal, the current count lit) appears on the count display above the pad, in the step card's progress, on the Lens icon and on the video title card.** It never appears on a button and never as decoration on the Devpost page's body text. Because it is the one shape that says what the product is without a word.

## Edges
19. **Every drawn edge is a capsule or a rounded end: cells, buttons, the pad's corners (4 cm radius), the arrow's stroke.** No square corners, no sharp points except the toes of a sole print. Because the claves have rounded ends and a slight taper.
