# Art direction: Floorcount

Light painting, long exposure, in a dark room.
One continuous stroke of ivory light, drawn by hand, with a soft tail where the light decays.
The line is thick where the stroke lands and thins as it rings out, like a struck clave.
A second light, pale teal-blue like a hall ceiling washed by a stage lamp, appears once per image, on one point only.
The ground is a plain dark floor, out of focus, nothing else in the room.
Colours: ivory, pale teal-blue, dark floor, nothing else.
Flat light from the stroke itself; no spotlight, no lens flare, no sparkle, no smoke.
Composition: one subject, centred low in the frame as if on the floor, seen from standing height, a little tilt.
Subjects are objects from a salsa class: a pair of claves, a dance-shoe sole print, a square of parquet floor, a strip of eight cells.
No people.

Never: text, letters, numbers, logos.
Never: neon tubes, glow halos, bokeh, lens flare, sparkles, smoke, haze, disco ball.
Never: magenta, purple, violet, pink, red, orange.
Never: 3D render, glossy plastic, chrome, isometric, vector clip art, cartoon.
Never: silhouettes of dancing couples, roses, flames, swirls.
Avoid: anything photographic of a real person or place.
