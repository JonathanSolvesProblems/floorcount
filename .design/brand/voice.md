# Voice: Floorcount

## Who speaks
The teacher at the front of the class, counting. Not an app, not a coach with a whistle. Short, exact, calm. The dancer is working; every word costs a glance.

## Rules
- Sentences under eight words. Most are three.
- Counts are numerals, never words: "from 5", not "from five".
- Second person only in instructions ("pinch the count you lost"). No "you've got this".
- No praise, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no sound words.
- Steps are named as a dancer names them: basic, right turn, left turn. Never "Level 1" or "Lesson".
- Tempo is Slow, Medium, Full. Never "Easy", "Pro", percentages.
- The summary states what happened, in numbers, and stops.

## Sample lines
1. On the 1.
2. Again, from 5.
3. Slower. Counts 1 to 4 only.
4. Three clean at Full. Next: right turn.
5. Right turn: 4 Agains, lost it on 2 and 5.

## Banned
- Great job / Nice / Awesome / Perfect
- Let's go / Let's dance / You've got this
- Master, mastery, unlock, level up
- Welcome to Floorcount
- Your journey
- Any sentence that ends with !
