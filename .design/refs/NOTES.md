# Found details (every photo viewed 2026-11-02)

Sources: Wikimedia Commons via commons_refs.py; credits in credits.json. Two fetches were off target (a harpsichord, a 1902 book page) and are ignored.

## The clave
- claves-instrument-1 and -2 (two maker's product photos, a cherry pair and a walnut pair; corrected 6 November: I first read these as one pair in two woods, and they are two separate pairs). Rounded ends, a slight taper. The maker's mark is burned into the wood inside an oval. Laid on weathered grey boards: a warm object on a worn floor.
- claves-instrument-3: the clave is cupped in the palm; the hollow of the hand is the resonator. Padauk, red-brown with speckled grain.
- claves-instrumento-4: hornbeam claves, pale cream wood, crossed in an X on a grey ground. The X is also how a clave stroke is written in percussion notation.
- Eyedrop, woods: #907f6b, #a98865, #aa9987, #978371 (hue 27 to 36), walnut #45372e, padauk #844e40 (hue 12).

## The floor
- baile-de-salsa-2 (salsa class, Iberoconf 2023): the dance floor is a portable parquet square laid over the hall's carpet, with a visible straight edge. Basket-weave parquet. Ceiling washed teal-blue (#01739e, #4486b2, hue 196 to 204). Dancers in golden-yellow shirts and lavender dresses. The floor is a square with a border: that is the pad.
- dance-studio-wooden-floor-0: whitewashed brick, pale oak boards, skylight, mirrors along one wall. Ivory #f7f4f0, oak #c1b3a3 and #a98865.

## The conventions, seen in the world
- baile-de-salsa-0 (band gig): magenta, violet and green stage haze, lit floor panels, a dip. The club look.
- baile-de-salsa-3 (event poster): couple silhouettes, swirls, magenta, red, yellow and blue, condensed caps. The poster look.
- baile-de-salsa-4 (LA-style basic diagram): foot blobs in peach and light blue with numerals inside, "Basic 1 / Basic 2". The footwork-diagram look, including the blue-and-orange default the brief carries. Not to be rebuilt.

## What carries forward
- The pad is a bordered square laid on the real floor (class photo), textured faintly like parquet (which also hides the additive display's unevenness, per Snap's guidance).
- Two feet, one pair, two roles: one clave is held in the cupped hand and the other strikes it (claves-instrument-3). So the feet differ in treatment, solid and outlined, not in cartoon colour.
- The strike: light on the count, decaying like a struck hardwood. Holds (4 and 8) are rests: the clave's unstruck boxes.
- The X: the written clave stroke, available as the mark.
- Ivory is the brightest thing in the studio and on the display.
